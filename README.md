## Overview

Expense Tracker is a Flutter mobile application that makes it effortless to log expenses, stay within a monthly budget, and see exactly where your money is going. It was designed with one goal: make the daily habit of tracking spending feel fast and frictionless, not like a chore.

The app uses a bottom navigation bar to keep four core screens always one tap away.

---

## Screens

### 🏠 Dashboard

The home screen gives you a complete picture of where you stand, at a glance.

- **Personalized greeting** — "Good afternoon, Layan"
- **Gradient summary card** — shows total spent ($163.50), monthly budget ($500.00), and remaining balance ($336.50) in a single hero element
- **Category cards** — horizontal scrollable row with spending per category (Food & Drink $47 · Transport $7 · Shopping $27)
- **Recent transactions** — a clean list of the latest expenses, showing amount, label, and category

### ➕ Add Expense

Accessible instantly from the central "+" tab — the fastest path to logging a new expense.

- Large amount input field
- Title / description field
- **Category chips** — tap to select: Food & Drink, Transport, Shopping, Bills
- Date field defaulting to today
- "Save Expense" button

The one-tap access from anywhere in the app keeps the friction of logging low enough to actually use it.

### 📊 Reports

Understand your spending patterns without reading a wall of numbers.

- **Category breakdown** with horizontal progress bars per category — chosen over a pie chart for mobile scannability
  - Food & Drink: $46.75
  - Bills: $65.00
  - Shopping: $27.00
  - Transport: $6.75
- **3-stat summary row**: Total Spent · Transactions · Avg/Item

### 👤 Profile

- Initials avatar (LK)
- Monthly Budget setting
- Notifications toggle
- Export Data
- Appearance preferences
- Log Out
